## Breadth, limits and architecture impact

Inspected Control endpoint, socket, closed envelope and close reactions, shared client pending/reconnect state, all five Marshall service wrappers, Lilly/Audi/Ziggy/Galileo adapters, hints and service links, credential lock path, Audi stream/catalog deadlines, ADR-0141/0142 and relevant TTL source. Existing ordinary regressions are cited in evidence but were not freshly executed; their presence is not a hosted assertion. A controlled TLS peer can test transport state, while domain-effect properties require real Marshall/PostgreSQL and independent durable, native, guest or upstream witnesses. Guest isolation and physical HA are outside this focus. The known IngressCatalog receiver range is a source-level policy mismatch, not an observed production stale route: current Marshall publishes 5,000 ms. Ordinary web/SSH/editor grants may last 30,000 ms; nested/catalog/DNS authority is limited to 5,000 ms by accepted policy, with nested also publication-bound.

This research writes only scratchbook evidence and does not change a protocol, application behavior, architecture decision or HLDD-covered interface. Architecture impact is therefore none at this stage; the listed contracts are proposed verification targets. Any implementation that changes their behavior needs a fresh architecture/HLDD review.
