# pool-contention-preserves-neighbor-progress

P1; medium-high confidence; progress and safety. Planned `Sometimes(condition)` message: **"Lilly independent pool mutation completes during sustained sampling pressure"**, where `condition` is a successful controlled neighbor mutation after an earlier sample/mutation overlap and within an explicitly configured observation window once fault injection stops. This is a meaningful progress state, not a tautological callback. Companion `Always` message: **"Lilly pool contention preserves retained placement and outcome"**, checked whenever acquisition returns `storage_busy`. Both proposed assertions are **missing** in the [fresh scan](../../existing-assertions.md).

Source: `lilly/lib/lilly/host/storage.ex:4-51` retries only lock contention before callback, gives mutations a one-second window, and makes observations zero-wait. `lilly/lib/lilly/host/pool_snapshot.ex:58-59` refuses a released snapshot. `lilly/lib/lilly/control/runtime.ex:252-296` uses one in-flight sample and drops intervening ticks; its source note records earlier back-to-back sampling starving relaunch. ADR-0152 documents four creates roughly three seconds apart and a narrower preparation design; `docs/architecture/reviews/2026-10-09-transient-pool-contention.md:16-28` distinguishes unavailable observation from physical absence. These historical observations motivate the schedule, not a claim that the current release passes.

Workload and independent oracle: run distinct VM create/delete/clone operations on a real pool while background capacity sample, template prewarm and standby refill are active. Keep a workload ledger of acknowledged placement/generation and a separate ZFS dataset/guest sentinel inventory; compare reservations and durable journal receipts after transient contention. Permit bounded `storage_busy` refusals. Once injected obstruction ceases and a pool lock is demonstrably available, a repeated neighbor request should eventually succeed under a chosen service deadline. Do not claim unconditional progress through permanent external obstruction. A SUT `Reachable` message **"Lilly storage mutation acquired lock after sampled contention"** can guide replay.

Value menu: 1/2/4/8 neighbors; sample 0/1/continuous; prewarm/refill on/off; lock hold below/near/above one second; one/two pools; queue with and without injected caller death. Hosted locks and modeled executor cover coordination only. Real ZFS capacity and clone effects require native or explicitly scoped TCG storage. Open question: what service deadline is justified by a native baseline at the configured load, rather than invented from the one-second acquisition retry?
