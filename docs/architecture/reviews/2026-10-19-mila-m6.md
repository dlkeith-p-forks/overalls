# Mila M6: integrated client, relay and verification (2026-10-19)

Scope: [ADR-0220](../decisions/0220-mila-client-over-porsche-ssh.md) through
[ADR-0228](../decisions/0228-strict-host-certificates-in-mila.md) on the
integrated `feat/mila` tree. That tree combines:

- the Mila client: M1–M5;
- the Porsche client commands, ReadMarker and `watch` (ADR-0222);
- Porsche terminal attach (ADR-0223);
- the Marshall Notifications domain (ADR-0225);
- the Susan relay (ADR-0224).

Boundaries reviewed at integration:

- **One protocol, one fixture per contract.** These are shared by Elixir,
  Dart and Swift:
  - command catalog: `OverallsProtocol.SSHCommand`;
  - routes: `AppRoute`;
  - E2E pokes: `PushPoke`;
  - Susan wire body: `SusanPoke`.

  Each side tests against the same fixture under `protocol/test/fixtures/` or
  `protocol/priv/`. The one cross-workstream divergence was found here and fixed
  in code: what the Susan signature covers. See the
  [Susan review](2026-10-19-susan-push-relay.md).
- **Authority stays in Marshall.** Mila holds no authority of its own. Every
  operation goes through a Porsche SSH session bound to a device key. Push pokes
  carry only a sealed route, and Mila fetches the details over SSH.
- **Merged shared surfaces.** The command catalog groups, `ssh/commands.ex`
  dispatch, `control_requests.ex` refusal mapping, the Porsche client reasons and
  runner hints, the gettext catalogs and the Events catalogs were combined by
  union. No workstream's entries were dropped.
- **Placement.** Susan stays outside every fleet host. Marshall's only new egress
  is the compiled Susan origin.

Decision status: all of ADR-0220–0228 remain `accepted`, implementation `partial`.
